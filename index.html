Installation

Up and running in under two minutes

No Chrome Web Store approval, no account, no drama. Just four steps.

1

Download the ZIP

Hit the download button. A file named attendance-manager-v1.1.zip lands in your Downloads folder.

2

Extract it

Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All on Windows, or just double-click it on Mac. You should see a folder with manifest.json inside.

3

Open chrome://extensions

Paste chrome://extensions into a new tab in Chrome, Edge or Brave, then switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

4

Load Unpacked

Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder — not the ZIP itself. Pin the extension, open your ERP once, and you are done.

Loading the ZIP itself will fail. Extract it first, then point Chrome at the folder that contains manifest.json.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The stuff people usually ask before installing.

Is Attendance Manager really free?
Yes, completely. No ads, no paywall, no premium tier, no hidden catch. If it saves you from a detention you can buy me a coffee, but that is genuinely optional.
Do I need to install it from the Chrome Web Store?
No. For now it is distributed as a ZIP and installed manually through Load Unpacked. It takes about a minute and works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. The steps above walk you through it.
Where is my attendance data stored?
Entirely on your laptop, inside your browser's local extension storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, there is no backend, no analytics and no account. Uninstall the extension and the data goes with it.
What happens when the college ERP changes its layout?
Because it reads the ERP page directly, a major ERP update can temporarily break the sync. Download the latest ZIP and reinstall with Load Unpacked. If it still looks off, ping me and I will patch it as soon as I can.
